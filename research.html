Research

What we work on

Seven connected research themes, grounded in our publication record—from generalizable neural decoding to assistive robotics and developmental neuroscience.

01→

Generalizable EEG Decoding

Developing models that transfer across people and reduce the need for individual calibration.

02→

Machine Learning for Neural Signals

Model selection, interpretable deep learning, and statistical methods for neural classification.

03→

BCIs for Communication & Interaction

P300, visual evoked potentials, and brain-driven spelling and gaming interfaces.

04→

Clinical NeuroAI & Epilepsy

EEG analysis for epilepsy, source imaging, and attention-related research.

05→

Neurorehabilitation & Assistive Robotics

Connecting EEG and EMG with exoskeletons, robots, and prosthetic devices.

06→

fNIRS & Hemodynamic Decoding

Measuring and classifying hemodynamic responses during cognitive and motor tasks.

07→

Developmental & Social Neuroscience

Collaborative work on neural decoding during natural infant behavior and social interaction.
