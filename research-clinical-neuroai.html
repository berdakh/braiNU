Clinical NeuroAI & Epilepsy

EEG analysis for epilepsy, source imaging, and attention-related research.

This line of work spans wavelet-based epileptic spike and seizure detection, EEG source imaging in partial epilepsy, and a review of deep learning for intracranial EEG. Earlier work also examines EEG-based decision support for ADHD. These studies are research contributions; they do not establish clinical deployment of a lab-developed system.

Research questions & methods

  • Epileptic spike detection and seizure analysis
  • EEG source imaging and presurgical evaluation
  • Intracranial EEG methods and attention-related EEG analysis
