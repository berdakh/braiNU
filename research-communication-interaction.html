BCIs for Communication & Interaction

P300, visual evoked potentials, and brain-driven spelling and gaming interfaces.

This research connects neural decoding with interfaces for communication and interaction. The publication record includes a Cyrillic P300 speller, ERP feature learning, and a systematic review of visual evoked potential interfaces in gaming.

Research questions & methods

  • P300 spelling and event-related potential decoding
  • Spatiospectral features for communication interfaces
  • Visual evoked potential BCIs and gaming
