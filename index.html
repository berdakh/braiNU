Our mission

Engineering intelligence at the interface between brains and machines.

braiNU brings together artificial intelligence, neural engineering, biomedical signal processing, robotics, and neuroscience to decode human brain activity and turn that knowledge into useful clinical and assistive systems.

✓Develop generalizable machine learning methods for EEG, EMG, fNIRS and intracranial EEG.
✓Advance brain-computer interfaces that require less subject-specific calibration.
✓Translate neural analytics into tools for epilepsy care, rehabilitation and human-machine interaction.
