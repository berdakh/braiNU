BMI Lab · Nazarbayev University

Neural engineering
and brain–machine intelligence.

The Brain–Machine Intelligence Lab develops artificial intelligence and machine-learning methods for interpreting neural data and advancing brain–machine systems. Our work examines robust representation learning, cross-subject generalisation, and multimodal decoding, with applications in brain–computer interfaces, epilepsy, neurorehabilitation, and intelligent robotics.

Research focus

Neural signals, computational methods, and assistive systems.

Research at BMI Lab centres on the development and evaluation of machine-learning methods for neural data, drawing on signal processing, statistical inference, and neural engineering.

✓AI and machine-learning methods for EEG, EMG, fNIRS, and intracranial EEG analysis.
✓Generalizable representation learning and cross-participant neural decoding.
✓Brain–machine systems for epilepsy research, neurorehabilitation, robotics, and human–machine interaction.
