Generalizable EEG Decoding

Developing models that transfer across people and reduce the need for individual calibration.

EEG varies between people and recording sessions. This line of work examines subject-independent decoding for motor imagery and event-related potentials, including approaches trained on limited numbers of participants.

Research questions & methods

  • Multisubject ensembles and cross-subject classification
  • Attention mechanisms and compact transformers
  • Learning with small subject pools and zero-calibration ERP approaches
