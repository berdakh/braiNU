Berdakh Abibullaev, PhD

I’m an Associate Professor at the School of Computing and AI, Nazarbayev University.

I lead braiNU, where we bring together machine learning, neural signal processing and brain–computer interfaces to study brain activity and develop clinical and assistive neurotechnology.

berdakh.abibullaev@nu.edu.kz

Research

My research focuses on decoding neural signals and building machine learning models that generalize across people and recording conditions. I work with EEG, intracranial EEG, EMG and fNIRS, with applications in epilepsy research, neurorehabilitation and human–machine interaction.

Our methods span deep learning, transformer models, transfer learning and domain adaptation. I’m particularly interested in connecting advances in neural decoding with useful assistive systems.

Teaching & mentorship

I teach undergraduate and graduate courses spanning machine learning, brain–machine interfaces, neural signal processing, signals and systems, and embedded systems. My teaching connects mathematical foundations with programming and practical engineering, and I mentor researchers working across AI and neural engineering.
