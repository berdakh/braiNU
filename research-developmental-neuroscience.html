Developmental & Social Neuroscience

Collaborative work on neural decoding during natural infant behavior and social interaction.

This earlier collaborative work investigates methods for recording and analyzing neural activity during freely behaving infant interactions. It includes an experimental and analytical protocol and conference reports on intent decoding and the development of social cognition.

Research questions & methods

  • Multimodal recording during natural social interaction
  • EEG classification of intended behaviors
  • Early developmental studies of social cognition
