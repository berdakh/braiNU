Machine Learning for Neural Signals

Model selection, interpretable deep learning, and statistical methods for neural classification.

Our methodological work explores how model architecture, training data, feature representations, and interpretability shape neural signal classification. It includes both deep learning studies and statistical classification methods.

Research questions & methods

  • Systematic CNN model selection for P300 and sensorimotor rhythms
  • Transformer architectures and data constraints
  • Layerwise relevance propagation and bias-corrected classifiers
